Key Design: Terminal Job Control

Ctrl+C must hit the job, never the shell

  • Shell puts itself in its own process group at startup
  • Each job gets its own process group (setpgid)
  • tcsetpgrp() hands the terminal to the foreground job, so Ctrl+C / Ctrl+Z are delivered to the job, not the shell
  • fg: grant terminal + SIGCONT; bg: SIGCONT only
  • No tty? (piped stdin) → shell skips job control, children share its group

Live behavior

$ sleep 30   then Ctrl+Z

[1] Stopped  sleep 30  PID: 4210

$ bg 1   → resumes in background

$ fg 1   → back to foreground
