Process Monitor

Reading the kernel’s /proc filesystem

What we read

  • /proc/[pid]/stat: state, PPID, utime/stime, threads, vsize, RSS
  • /proc/[pid]/cmdline: NUL separated argv
  • /proc/meminfo, /proc/uptime: header summary

Parsed after the last ) so process names with spaces and parentheses are safe. CPU % = tick delta between two snapshots 200 ms apart.
